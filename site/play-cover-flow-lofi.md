# Cover Flow Lofi

The lighter Cover Flow renderer: projective covers, reflections, and fast nearest-neighbor album sampling.

Controls:

- Drag left/right: move through albums with inertia
- Tap an album: select it
- Keyboard: `Left/Right` or `A/D`
