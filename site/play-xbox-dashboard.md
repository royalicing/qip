# Original Xbox Dashboard

An interactive dashboard sketch inspired by the original Xbox menu flow.

Controls:

- `Up` / `Down`: move selection
- `Enter` or `A`: open category
- `Esc` or `B`: back
- Pointer hover and click are also supported
